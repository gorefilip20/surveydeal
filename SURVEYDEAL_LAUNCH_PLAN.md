# SurveyDeal Launch Plan

## Positioning

SurveyDeal should own a narrow, useful category: **the deal layer for digital work**. The product is not “another crypto wallet” and should not lead with blockchain jargon. The emotional promise is simpler: good work deserves a better deal. The functional proof is a shared agreement, protected funding, milestone evidence, and a visible release path.

The first audience should be freelancers, small product studios, agencies, DAO contributors, and founders who already do paid digital work across borders. These users feel the pain of unclear scope, late approvals, and informal payment promises, but they do not need a broad financial platform to understand the value.

## Monetization

The pilot model is a **1% success fee on released funds**, with no monthly subscription. This keeps the product aligned with customer value and makes the price easy to explain before signing. The fee should be calculated and displayed before the buyer funds a deal, recorded in the receipt, and capped according to the deployed contract configuration.

Once the core loop is proven, add three expansion lines:

| Offer | Buyer | Pricing direction |
|---|---|---|
| Team workspaces | Agencies and small studios | Monthly workspace fee plus lower transaction rate |
| API and webhooks | Marketplaces, OTC desks, and payroll platforms | Usage-based API fee plus volume tier |
| Managed dispute / operations | Teams with high-value or time-sensitive work | Per-case operations fee with explicit service terms |

Do not monetize custody, hidden spreads, or unclear wallet routing. Trust is the product advantage; opaque pricing would destroy it.

## Acquisition funnel

1. **Founder-led proof:** publish short deal breakdowns showing how an unclear freelance project becomes a funded, milestone-based agreement.
2. **Partner distribution:** recruit five small agencies and three DAO contributor communities for a closed pilot. Give each partner a reusable deal template and a referral link.
3. **Template-led SEO:** create practical pages for “freelance escrow,” “agency milestone payments,” “stablecoin contractor payments,” and “how to structure a digital project deposit.” Each page should point to the same deal builder.
4. **Conversion event:** optimize for “create a deal,” not newsletter signups. The first-run flow should ask for the counterparty, deliverables, amount, and milestones before asking for optional advanced settings.
5. **Retention loop:** after completion, issue a shareable receipt and prompt both parties to reuse the template for their next deal. Completed-deal count, response time, and dispute rate can become the foundation of reputation features.

## Product message hierarchy

Lead with clarity and outcomes. Explain blockchain only where it changes the user’s decision. Show exact network, token, fee, funding state, and release state. Never describe unverified deployments, unsupported adapters, or unaudited contracts as production-ready.

## Safety gates before real-money launch

The product should remain a controlled pilot until the repository has meaningful contract tests, an external security review, a verified testnet deployment, an explicit contract address and chain list, production database backups, RPC failover, admin rate limiting, and a published incident/support path. The public UI should continue to distinguish payment wallets from smart-contract escrow funding.

## 30-day operating target

Aim for 10 pilot teams, 25 completed deals, less than 10% dispute rate, at least 70% of new deals created from a reusable template, and a median time-to-create under five minutes. These metrics validate whether SurveyDeal is reducing friction rather than merely attracting curious wallet users.
